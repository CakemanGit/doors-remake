# DOORS Remake 👁️

An atmospheric 3D horror game inspired by Roblox DOORS, built using React Three Fiber, TypeScript, and Vite. 

## 🎮 Key Features
* **Procedural Dungeon Generation:** Rooms and corridors spawn dynamically with seamless coordinate stitching, preventing any geometry clipping.
* **Realistic Interactions:** Immersive first-person controls with automatic Pointer Lock. Approach doors or wardrobes and press **"E"** to interact.
* **Working Inventory HUD:** Use items directly from your screen layout. Activate your flashlight to beam narrow SpotLight cones or consume Vitamins (**VIT**) to boost your walking speed.
* **Smart Rear Doors Mechanic:** Opened doors remain physically unmounted and wide open behind your back, letting you track your progress just like in the original game.
* **Rush AI Entity Included:** C# WebSockets infrastructure and frontend triggers ready for the ultimate monstrous threat.


## 🛠️ Controls
* **WASD** / Arrow Keys — Move around the hotel rooms
* **Mouse** — Look around (Pointer Lock engages automatically)
* **LMB (Left Click)** / **E** — Interact with doors and hide in wardrobes
* **Flashlight** — Turn the light source on/off via the HUD button
